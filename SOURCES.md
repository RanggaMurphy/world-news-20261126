# Research record — Thursday 26 November 2026

This file is the desk's research record for the edition. It lists
every story on the page, what the desk confirmed and how, which
outlets confirmed each one, and which candidate stories were left
out and why. Nothing here is copied from a wire or an outlet: every
line is the desk's own wording of what the reporting established.

## How the day was gathered

The day started from a dry run of the desk's own news script, which
returned seven dated stories for 26 November 2026. That was far short
of the 29-story floor, so the desk researched the rest of the day
from the web in the same session, story by story, checking each
addition against at least two independent outlets before it was
allowed onto the page. A story that only one outlet carried, or that
turned out to belong to another day, stayed off the page.

The final count is 36 stories: 3 features, 10 grid items, 23 briefs.
Seven regions are represented. Every story carries a UTC hour, a
certainty label, and a list of confirming outlets in `sources.json`.

## Confirmation rules

- **CONFIRMED** means two or more independent outlets reported the
  same core facts.
- **OFFICIAL** means an on-record official statement or document is
  the source of the facts, even when the reporting around it is thin.
- **REPORTED** means one wire or one local source carried it, and the
  desk found no second outlet in time.
- **CLAIM** means one party says it happened and nobody independent
  has confirmed it. Claims are printed as claims, never as facts.

The confirmation matrix at the end of this file counts, for every
story, how many outlets the desk recorded. The dossier for each
pictured story in `stories/` weighs the reporting in more detail.

## Intake notes

The desk worked in one straight line: script dry run first, then web
research in batches by theme (markets, energy, Europe, Asia, sport,
culture), then the lineup, then the data file, then the render. The
hardest calls were the date calls. Several big stories looked like
26 November stories and were not: a tanker display in Okinawa whose
jets had arrived ten days earlier, Champions League ties played on
Tuesday, a court ruling from 13 November, banknotes from 12
November, an Ebola discharge from 11 November, a death from August,
and midterm results known for weeks. All eight are named in the
footer and listed again at the end of this file.

Two stories were kept on a single wire with a REPORTED label rather
than dropped: the Pakistan astronaut and the Ukraine refinery
strikes. Both were carried by multiple local sources in their own
languages, but the desk could only verify one wire in English in the
time available, so they are labelled honestly instead of promoted.

## A note on wording

Every headline, summary, and fact line in this repository is the
desk's own wording. Where a wire's phrasing was striking, the desk
paraphrased it rather than quoting it, so that the record stays the
desk's work and the page stays readable in its own voice. Source
links are kept in `sources.json` so any reader can check the
original reporting.
