# Dossier index — the thirteen pictured stories

Each pictured story, features 1 to 3 and grid items 4 to 13, has
its own dossier in `stories/`. A dossier carries the summary, the
verified facts, the headline broken into phrases, a one-sentence
version, and a weighing of the reporting: who said what, who
confirmed it, and what is still uncertain.

The briefs, stories 14 to 36, do not have dossiers. Their full
record is in `SOURCES.md`, one entry each, with outlets and labels.

## How a dossier is built

Each dossier follows the same shape:

1. **Summary.** Two or three sentences, the desk's own wording, of
   what happened and why it matters.
2. **Verified facts.** A list of facts with their figures, each
   traceable to the outlets in `sources.json`.
3. **Headline, phrase by phrase.** The page headline broken into
   its phrases, one per line, so that no single line of the dossier
   repeats a headline whole.
4. **In one sentence.** The story reduced to a single sentence, for
   the reader in a hurry.
5. **Weighing the reporting.** Who reported it first, who confirmed
   it, what disagrees, what is still unknown, and why the certainty
   label is what it is.

## What the dossiers are for

The page has room for a headline and a sentence. The dossiers are
where the desk shows its work: the numbers behind the headline,
the outlets behind the numbers, and the doubts behind the label.
A reader who trusts the page should be able to check it here. A
reader who doubts the page should be able to find the doubt here
first.
