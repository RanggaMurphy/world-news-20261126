# Dossier 12 — Solomon Islands and Australia

Story 12 of 36. Grid. Region: Oceania. Pin: Honiara, Solomon
Islands. Certainty: CONFIRMED. UTC hour: 02:00.

## Summary

Solomon Islands signed a security treaty making Australia its
partner of choice and ruling out any foreign base. Canberra will
fund policing, schools, and infrastructure over a decade as the
islands turn away from Beijing. The treaty was signed in Canberra;
the story belongs to the islands, so the pin sits on the islands.

## Verified facts

- The treaty was signed on Wednesday in Canberra.
- Australia becomes the islands' primary security partner.
- No foreign military base is allowed.
- The funding is 740 million dollars over a decade.
- Of that, 670 million is grants and 70 million is loans.
- The funding covers policing, infrastructure, education, and
  sport.
- The treaty needs ratification to take effect.
- The 2022 China pact was reported not to be renewed, but the
  prime minister would not confirm that, citing a
  non-disclosure agreement.
