# Dossier 8 — United States tariffs on 14 economies

Story 8 of 36. Grid. Region: North America. Pin: Washington,
United States. Certainty: OFFICIAL.

## Summary

The United States trade office proposed Section 301 duties on 14
economies over excess factory capacity. Taiwan faces up to 15
percent, China up to 20 percent, with higher fallbacks if talks
fail. Comments are due 5 January 2027 and a hearing is set for
25 January 2027. A separate 10 percent duty on Brazilian Embraer
aircraft was also proposed.

## Verified facts

- The proposal covers 14 economies: Bangladesh, Cambodia, China,
  the European Union, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Malaysia,
  Singapore, Switzerland, Taiwan, Thailand, and Vietnam.
- The caps are 15 percent for the EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland,
  and Taiwan.
- The caps are 18 percent for Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia,
  Thailand, and Bangladesh.
- China faces 20 percent, with a 40 percent fallback if the truce
  fails.
- Vietnam faces 20 percent, with a 35 percent fallback.
- India faces 17 percent, with a 25 percent fallback.
- Comments are due 5 January 2027; the hearing is 25 January 2027.
- A separate 10 percent duty on Brazilian Embraer aircraft was
  proposed.
