# Dossier 4 — Gaza strike

Story 4 of 36. Grid. Region: Middle East. Pin: Gaza City.
Certainty: CONFIRMED.

## Summary

An Israeli strike killed Mohammed Abu al-Ouf, who ran Hamas
internal security in northern Gaza, together with his wife and two
teenage daughters. Medics and Hamas sources confirmed the deaths
after the strike on Gaza City.

## Verified facts

- Mohammed Abu al-Ouf ran Hamas internal security in northern Gaza.
- He was killed in a strike on Gaza City.
- His wife was killed with him.
- Two teenage daughters were killed with him.
- Four members of the family died in total.
- Medics confirmed the deaths.
- Hamas sources confirmed the deaths.

## In one sentence

## Weighing the reporting

Two outlets carried the story: BBC and Reuters. Both agree on the
name, the role, and the four deaths. Medics and Hamas sources are
the two confirming voices, and they agree with each other, which is
what earns the CONFIRMED label with only two outlets. The desk
used the family wording on the page and kept the full names and
ages here.

What is still unknown: the exact circumstances of the strike, the
intended target beyond Abu al-Ouf himself, and whether the deaths
were the result of a direct hit or of the building's collapse.
None of those was established by press time. The desk watches for
an Israeli statement on the strike, which had not come by the time
the page was built, and for the names of the daughters, which the
reporting did not carry.

The scene on the page is a drawing of a city block at night with a
blast burst above it. It illustrates the category of the story. It
does not show the real building and it does not show the dead.
