# Dossier 7 — Bangladesh garment pay

Story 7 of 36. Grid. Region: Asia. Pin: Dhaka, Bangladesh.
Certainty: CONFIRMED.

## Summary

Bangladesh lifted the minimum wage for garment workers by 36
percent after weeks of street protests. Unions said the rise still
missed what workers asked for, and fresh protests flared. The page
carries the rise; the argument about whether it is enough is here.

## Verified facts

- Bangladesh raised the garment minimum wage by 36 percent.
- The decision followed weeks of street protests.
- Unions said the rise still missed what workers asked for.
- Fresh protests flared after the announcement.
- The garment sector is the country's largest export earner.
- The decision was announced on 26 November 2026.

## Weighing the reporting

Two outlets carried the story: AP and Reuters. Both agree on the 36
percent figure and on the sequence, protests then decision then more
protests. Two outlets is the minimum for CONFIRMED, and the desk
accepts it here because the figure is a government decision, which
is a matter of record, and because both wires agree on it.

What is still unknown: the wage figure in local currency and what
it buys, the unions' exact demand, and whether the fresh protests
will force a second rise. The desk watches the next round of talks
between the government, the factory owners, and the unions. The
story is not over; the page records the day's decision, not the
settlement.
