# Dossier 2 — Houthi claim on a Saudi refinery

Story 2 of 36. Feature. Region: Middle East. Pin: Ras Tanura,
Saudi Arabia. Certainty: CLAIM. UTC hour: 05:00.

## Summary

Yemen's Houthis said they struck the Ras Tanura refinery and
Riyadh's financial district. Pictures from above showed a wide
smoke plume. Riyadh said its defences downed four drones and did
not confirm a direct hit. Brent crude rose past 94 dollars a barrel,
up about 3.5 percent. The story is second on the page because it
moved the oil price and because it is a claim, which is exactly why
it carries a CLAIM chip.

## Verified facts

- The Houthi spokesman announced strikes on Ras Tanura and Riyadh.
- The refinery's capacity is 550,000 barrels a day.
- Satellite pictures showed a large smoke plume.
- Saudi Arabia said four drones were downed.
- Riyadh did not confirm a direct hit on the refinery.
- A coalition strike hit Hodeidah port in response.
- Brent crude reached 94.61 dollars, up about 3.5 percent.
- The claim and the market move were reported by six outlets.

## Weighing the reporting

Six outlets reported the claim: Reuters, CGTN America, Firstpost,
Dhaka Tribune, Democracy Now, Middle East Eye. All six agree that
the claim was made and that the oil price rose. None of them
confirms a direct hit on the refinery. The smoke plume in the
satellite pictures is real, but a plume is not a hit: it could come
from a fire elsewhere in the facility, from a downed drone, or from
the response strike. The desk prints the claim as a claim, in red,
and prints the market move as a fact, because the market move is
confirmed and the hit is not.

What is still unknown: whether any drone reached the refinery, what
burned, whether production was affected, and whether the coalition
strike on Hodeidah port was retaliation or routine. The desk
watches for an independent assessment of the damage, which is the
only thing that would lift this story from CLAIM to CONFIRMED.

The scene on the page is a drawing of a refinery with a flare
stack. It illustrates the category of the story. It does not show
the real refinery and it does not show any damage.
