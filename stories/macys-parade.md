# Dossier 6 — Macy's parade delayed

Story 6 of 36. Grid. Region: North America. Pin: New York, United
States. Certainty: CONFIRMED.

## Summary

New York's Thanksgiving parade was put off to Saturday because of
high winds, the first postponement in its history. Forecasters
warned the giant balloons could not safely fly past the city's
towers. The parade has flown for a century without ever being
postponed; wind did what a century could not.

## Verified facts

- The Macy's Thanksgiving parade was delayed to Saturday.
- The reason was high winds.
- Winds were forecast at 12 to 25 miles per hour.
- The forecast warned the giant balloons could not safely fly
  past the city's towers.
- This is the first postponement in the parade's history.
- The new date is Saturday.
