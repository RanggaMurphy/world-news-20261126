# Dossier 13 — Pakistan's first astronaut

Story 13 of 36. Grid. Region: Asia. Pin: Beijing, China.
Certainty: REPORTED.

## Summary

Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali will become the first foreign
astronaut to visit China's Tiangong space station, joining the
Shenzhou-24 crew. It is a first for the Chinese programme, which
has never flown a foreign astronaut before. The story is labelled
REPORTED, not CONFIRMED, because no wire carried it in English in
the time available.

## Verified facts

- Muhammad Zeeshan Ali is the astronaut named.
- He is Pakistani.
- He will visit China's Tiangong space station.
- He will be the first foreign astronaut on the station.
- He joins the Shenzhou-24 crew.
- The mission is a first for the Chinese programme.
- Three secondary outlets agree on the name and the mission.
