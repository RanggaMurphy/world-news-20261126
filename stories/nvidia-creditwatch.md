# Dossier 9 — Nvidia on downgrade watch

Story 9 of 36. Grid. Region: North America. Pin: Santa Clara,
United States. Certainty: CONFIRMED. UTC hour: 18:56.

## Summary

S and P placed Nvidia's AA credit rating on watch for a cut, citing
governance risks after the chief's exit and return. A one-notch cut
is the likely outcome of a 90-day review, and shares fell about 4
percent. The story is kept separate from the market story even
though Nvidia's fall feeds both: this one is about the rating, that
one is about the day.

## Verified facts

- S and P placed Nvidia's AA rating on CreditWatch Negative.
- The review runs 90 days.
- A one-notch cut to AA-minus is the likely outcome.
- The reason cited is governance risk after the chief's exit and
  return.
- The chief resigned on 11 November and was reinstated on 22
  November.
- Moody's kept its Aa1 rating, with the outlook moved to stable
  from positive on 18 November.
- United States officials told Congress there was no Chinese
  involvement in the chief's affairs.
- Shares fell about 4 percent on the day.
