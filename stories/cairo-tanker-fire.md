# Dossier 1 — Cairo tanker fire

Story 1 of 36. Feature. Region: Africa. Pin: Cairo, Egypt.
Certainty: CONFIRMED. UTC hour: 07:00.

## Summary

A fuel tanker turned over on the ring road around the Egyptian
capital and burst into flames. At least 31 people died and 21 were
hurt, the health ministry said. Cars and motorbikes caught near the
tanker were left charred. It is the day's largest confirmed death
toll from a single incident, which is why it leads the page.

## Verified facts

- The tanker overturned on the ring road around Cairo.
- The tanker caught fire after overturning.
- At least 31 people died.
- At least 21 people were injured.
- The figures come from the health ministry.
- Cars and motorbikes near the tanker were burned.
- The fire was reported by seven outlets within hours.
- The toll in the numbers strip is 31 dead.

## Weighing the reporting

Seven outlets carried the story: AP, BBC, Guardian, Reuters, CNN,
New York Times, Sky News. That is the widest confirmation of any
story in this edition, and it is why the label is CONFIRMED without
hesitation. Early wires carried lower tolls, and the desk waited for
the health ministry's figure before locking the numbers strip. The
ministry is the authority on the dead and injured, and its figure is
the one the page prints.

What is still unknown: the cause of the overturn, the identity of
the dead, and whether the road was closed for long. None of those
was established by press time, and none of them is on the page.
The desk watches for the cause, which will decide whether the
story grows into an inquiry about road safety or stays a single
tragic accident.

The scene on the page is a drawing of a tanker with flames and
smoke. It illustrates the category of the story. It does not show
the real tanker, and it does not show the dead.

## Sources in detail

Seven outlets carried the story, and each added something. AP
moved first with the overturn and the fire. The BBC carried the
health ministry's toll and the detail of the charred cars and
motorbikes. The Guardian confirmed the toll independently. Reuters
carried the ministry figure and the ring road location. CNN added
the injury count. The New York Times carried the toll and the
ministry as the source. Sky News confirmed the fire and the road
closure. No outlet disagreed with any other on the toll, which is
unusual for a breaking story and is why the desk trusts the figure.

## What the desk watches next

The cause of the overturn, which will decide whether the story
grows into an inquiry about road safety on the ring road. The
identities of the dead, which the ministry has not yet released.
Whether the road was closed for long, and whether the fire spread
beyond the tanker. The desk will check the toll again before the
next edition, because tolls move after the first day.
